# Calibration Cases

These examples describe expected behavior. Re-evaluate if source facts change.

## Case 1: Director, IT Business Partner - Enterprise Applications
Behavior: Strong enterprise-applications, integration, ITSM, change, and executive-stakeholder alignment. Material gaps in full-lifecycle ERP implementation and healthcare/Corporate Services platforms. Expected decision: INVESTIGATE / NETWORK, approximately low 70s.

## Case 2: Technical Delivery Manager - AI & Cloud Platforms
Behavior: Strong delivery, cross-functional coordination, APIs, cloud, production readiness, incidents, and executive communication. Gaps in greenfield delivery, staffing/resource allocation, and production AI-platform delivery. Expected decision: APPLY, approximately low 80s, subject to work authorization and location feasibility.

## Anti-example: Broken URL with title only
Behavior: Return INSUFFICIENT DATA. Do not score from title or URL slug.
