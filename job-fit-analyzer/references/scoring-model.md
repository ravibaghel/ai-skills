# Scoring Model

## General Rules

- Start at zero and award demonstrated points.
- Score responsibilities before title.
- Concrete project evidence is stronger than keyword overlap.
- Required qualifications carry more weight than preferred qualifications.
- Do not double-penalize one gap.
- Final score is bounded from 0 to 100.

## Evidence Contribution Guidance

For a dimension or requirement:
- A: award roughly 85–100% of available credit.
- B: award roughly 65–85%.
- C: award roughly 30–60%.
- D: award 0%.
- X: award 0% and apply a conflict penalty or override when material.

Use judgment within ranges according to relevance, recency, scope, and strength of evidence.

## Penalty Guidance

Use proportional penalties, not automatic fixed penalties:
- Unsupported preferred qualification: usually 0 to -2.
- Unsupported non-core responsibility: usually -1 to -4.
- Unsupported explicit required qualification: usually -4 to -10.
- Core mandatory experience minimum not met: usually -8 to -20.
- Critical practical conflict: override to SKIP when confirmed.

## Thresholds
- 85–100: PRIORITY APPLY
- 75–84: APPLY
- 65–74: INVESTIGATE / NETWORK
- 0–64: SKIP
