# Evidence Guardrails

Company research and career evidence are separate evidence domains.

A company fact does not prove Ravi has that experience. Ravi evidence must come from the Career Evidence Bank or new user confirmation.

A job posting mentioning a technology is a ROLE SIGNAL, not proof that the technology is deployed company-wide.

A vendor case study describes only the use case and scope stated in that case study.

Never convert adjacent career evidence into equivalence. Examples:
- WideOrbit migration is not ERP implementation.
- Seaware COTS experience is not healthcare-domain experience.
- AWS involvement is not full cloud-platform ownership.
- GenAI PoC evaluation is not production AI delivery.
