# Company Research Brief

## Executive Brief
- Company and role
- Research purpose
- 4–6 most decision-relevant findings
- Why Ravi may be relevant
- Biggest uncertainty

## Company Context Relevant to This Role
Each item must identify VERIFIED FACT, ROLE SIGNAL, ANALYST INTERPRETATION, or UNKNOWN.

## Current Developments
Include date, source, relevance to role. Omit irrelevant news.

## Technology and Application Signals
Separate verified company facts from role-specific signals and unknowns.

## What This Could Mean for the Role
Clearly label all implications as ANALYST INTERPRETATION.

## Ravi Evidence Mapping
For each relevant theme:
- company/role theme
- Ravi evidence
- evidence strength: direct / supported / transferable
- best story: Seaware, WideOrbit, Technical Lead, or other approved evidence

## Gaps and Risks
Do not hide domain, technology, leadership, or practical gaps.

## Talking Points
3–5 evidence-backed talking points Ravi can naturally use.

## Questions for Recruiter
Prioritize scope, work arrangement, process, constraints, and unresolved requirements.

## Questions for Hiring Manager
Prioritize outcomes, application/platform landscape, delivery challenges, stakeholders, governance, and success measures.

## Interview Preparation Themes
List 3–6 themes and which evidence story supports each.

## Unknowns to Resolve
List important facts that public research could not verify.

## Sources
For each source include title, publisher/domain, publication/update date when available, URL/reference, and which findings it supports.
