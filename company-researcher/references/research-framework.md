# Research Framework

## Core Questions
1. What does the company explicitly say it does?
2. What part of the business appears relevant to this role?
3. What responsibilities and success themes does the official job posting emphasize?
4. What current company developments may materially affect the role?
5. What technology/application signals can be verified?
6. Which findings map directly to Ravi's evidence?
7. Which findings expose a gap or risk?
8. What should Ravi ask to resolve important unknowns?

## Research Layers
### Layer 1: Company
Business, products/services, stated strategy, relevant markets.
### Layer 2: Role
Responsibilities, qualifications, leadership model, outcome expectations.
### Layer 3: Current context
Dated announcements, filings, technology initiatives, partnerships, acquisitions, regulatory developments.
### Layer 4: Ravi relevance
Direct evidence, transferable evidence, gaps, talking points.
### Layer 5: Action
Recruiter questions, hiring-manager questions, interview themes, networking hooks.
