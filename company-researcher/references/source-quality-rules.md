# Source Quality Rules

## Tier 1: Primary
Official job posting, corporate site, investor relations, regulatory filings, newsroom, engineering/technology blog, official executive announcements.

## Tier 2: Strong secondary
Established business, technology, industry, or financial publications with named reporting and dates.

## Tier 3: Context only
Vendor/customer case studies and conference content. These can establish the described relationship/use case, but should not be generalized beyond what they state.

## Avoid as authority
Scraped job boards when the official posting exists, anonymous reviews, forums, unsourced social posts, SEO farms, automatically generated company profiles.

## Conflicts
If reliable sources conflict, report the conflict. Prefer the freshest authoritative primary source for current company facts, but do not erase historical context.
