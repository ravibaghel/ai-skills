# Strong Research Pattern

VERIFIED FACT: The official job posting states the role coordinates cloud, APIs, integrations, data, security, testing, and production readiness.

ROLE SIGNAL: The posting requests AWS and observability knowledge. This supports discussing Ravi's AWS production involvement and Splunk/CloudWatch/Grafana/AppDynamics evidence. It does not prove the employer uses Ravi's exact toolset across the company.

ANALYST INTERPRETATION: Because the role spans application development through production readiness, Ravi's Seaware 24A release and two-week hypercare story may be a stronger interview example than a generic cloud-technology discussion.

UNKNOWN: The public posting does not establish the direct-report structure. Ask the hiring manager whether team leadership is matrixed, delivery-based, or formal people management.
