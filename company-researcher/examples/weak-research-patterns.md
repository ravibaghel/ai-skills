# Weak Research Patterns to Avoid

Bad: "The company is migrating all applications to AWS."
Why: One AWS-related posting does not prove a company-wide migration.

Bad: "The hiring manager needs someone to fix poor delivery."
Why: Reason the role is open is unknown unless explicitly stated.

Bad: "Their culture is excellent."
Why: Marketing language or awards alone do not establish employee experience.

Bad: "Ravi has healthcare enterprise platform experience because Seaware is business-critical."
Why: Business-critical COTS experience does not create healthcare-domain experience.

Bad: "The team has 11 direct reports."
Why: A posting describing a team of 11 does not establish reporting lines.

Bad: "This recent initiative started last year."
Why: Never infer dates or duration not stated by a source.
