# Role Positioning

## Application Manager
Headline themes: enterprise applications, COTS lifecycle, integrations, ITSM, reliability.
Emphasize Seaware lifecycle, releases, incidents, support, vendor exposure, WideOrbit migration, and business/IT stakeholders.

## Technical Delivery Manager
Headline themes: technical delivery, enterprise applications, cloud/API integrations, production readiness.
Emphasize year-long Seaware release, 8–10 participant coordination, risk/dependencies/releases, hypercare, and four-phase WideOrbit cutover.

## Information Technology Manager
Headline themes: IT operations, enterprise applications, service delivery, cloud.
Emphasize ITSM, executive stakeholders, reliability, enterprise applications, vendors, and AWS involvement. Do not imply budget or formal line management.

## Information System Manager
Headline themes: enterprise systems, business integrations, lifecycle management.
Emphasize Seaware and WideOrbit as business systems, API/data integrations, application lifecycle, reliability, and modernization.

## Software Engineering Manager
Headline themes: engineering leadership, architecture, APIs, cloud, delivery.
Emphasize engineering foundation, architecture, development, mentoring, interviews, Agile/SDLC, and technical leadership. Do not imply performance management or direct reports.

## Technical Services Manager
Headline themes: service delivery, ITSM, production operations, reliability.
Emphasize SLAs, incidents, problem/RCA, production support, releases, monitoring, escalations, and hypercare.
