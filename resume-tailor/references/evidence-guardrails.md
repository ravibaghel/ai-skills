# Evidence Guardrails

## Evidence Classes
A: direct evidence. B: supported but incomplete. C: transferable, not equivalent. D: gap. X: conflict.

Only A and B may be stated as established experience. C should inform positioning but must not become an equivalent claim.

## Prohibited Inflation
- Cross-functional coordination to direct reports.
- Technical interviews to hiring authority.
- Mentoring to performance management.
- Schedule/dependency/risk planning to staffing/resource allocation.
- Application delivery to budget ownership.
- Deployment coordination to CI/CD architecture.
- AWS participation to full cloud architecture ownership.
- Migration to greenfield delivery.
- PoC evaluation to production AI delivery.
- COTS/integrations to ERP, SAP, Workday, healthcare, or regulated-domain expertise.

## Source Trace
Every materially changed bullet must cite a project or approved source in the Evidence Trace. Untraceable claims must be removed or marked for confirmation.
